How it works

  1. Sense

    Each creature sees the world through a fan of rays that pick up food, poison, walls and other creatures.

  2. Think

    A small neural network turns what it sees, and what it remembers, into turning and speed.

  3. Evolve

    Creatures that eat well have children with slightly changed brains. Over many generations, better behaviour takes over.
