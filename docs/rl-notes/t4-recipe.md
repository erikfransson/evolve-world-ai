- 2026-10-21. Read `agg/notes.md`, the training logs (lg8, lg8v2, lg4, la6, la12, thinker1, j50, jaug, j0, p50) and the founderEval logs (l10-la6, l10-la12, ls, ls0, live1, gj, tri25, thk, r0, r5, s25), `agg/rlTrain.ts`, `src/founderEval.ts`, `src/sim/rl/env.ts`, `server/src/config.ts` (all in the agg worktree), and docs/rl-grad-notes.md, rl-algo-notes.md, tuning-notes.md.
- How "held-out" is measured (T4-1, T4-2). With `--sample 8` rlTrain draws 8 envs once from the run seed (`deriveSeed(seed, 0x4e1d)`) and scores them on 12 seeds `HELD_BASE + k`. Training draws from the same env list, so the held-out envs are trained on too; only the seeds are held out. lg8 (seed 101) and lg8v2 (seed 111) were scored on disjoint subsets and against different rival libraries (lib-t vs lib-lean), so "lg8 3.87 -> lg8v2 6.34" is not a gain of 2.5. The same genome scored 3.87 and 5.70.
- Noise (T4-3, T4-4). Held-out sd between checks in the second half of each run: lg8v2 0.69, lg4-B 0.84, thinker1 0.67, j0 0.44, jaug 0.40, la12-B 0.36, lg8-B 0.37, la6-B 0.28, j50 0.26, p50 0.19. The fixed 12 seeds make each check a 96-episode reading of a moving genome, so most of the spread is real policy drift at sigma 0.05, not seed noise. The run end is the last check, and the founder pool is the top 31 of 93 perturbations ranked on the same 12 seeds (winner's curse: lg8v2 pool 8.18 to 6.66 around a 6.34 mean genome).
- Upkeep (T4-7, T4-8). Base upkeep per second is livingCost 2 + 0.02 x rays x length / 100 + 0.005 x (hidden + memory) (`sim/sensors.ts` line 126): lean 1x8 2.31, 1x6 2.30, 1x12 2.33, Explorer and Grazer 2.40, Juggernaut 2.44, Oracle 2.97. Lean saves about 0.09/s (4%) over the Explorer, while thrust costs up to 4/s. Oracle pays 24% more than lean and survives at bonus 10 where lean dies, so brain upkeep is not why lean founders fail. Upkeep decides only between equal behaviours: the grown Grazer (same acts, about 6% more) loses 3 to 327 (thk), and la12 (v 69, 2.33) outgrows the Explorer (v 64, 2.40) at bonus 0 (ls0: 1333/1337 vs 0/0). So "lean wins by upkeep" holds for la12 only because it is also fast.
- Live speed (T4-6). Mean speed of the living lineage at +2 min: lg8 19-31, lg8v2 22-24, la6 23-31; survivors Explorer 63-69, Oracle 73-74, Grazer (shipped) 62-71, Juggernaut 75-77, la12 69. The crash comes at +5 to +30 min (after the start boom, toward mid-winter at +30), and the fast lineages dip and recover while the slow ones do not.

- Reading. On a common benchmark the RL score does not predict Live; among the lean creatures it anti-predicts (lg8v2 tops the Grazer set and dies first). Two things the envs miss explain the table without invoking weakness of learning. (1) The envs score one individual over 60 to 90 s with survive and energy terms, plus four energy-saving envs (wait, lean-times, endurance, crowd), so a slow creature that idles near a fresh patch scores well. Live scores a lineage over hours at a food level the population itself has grazed down, where income per minute (and so births per minute) decides, and covering ground is what pays. (2) Live success is relative to the cast (T4-9): a founder that outgrows its rivals by a few percent of net intake takes over (competitive exclusion), so absolute RL score cannot be the selection measure. Crowding density in the inland envs is close to Live (13 rivals on 1300 x 1300, about 7.7e-6 per unit area, vs 3000 on the Inland Sea, 5.3e-6 before counting water; unverified how much land), so crowding is not the missing feature. Births, the grazed-down food level and the 60 min season are missing. Poison 50 and kill bonus 5 are already in the lean env files.
- Not genuine weakness of the learner. ES did what it was asked: lean brains learned the env optimum (slow, careful) quickly. The objective was wrong for Live, and the noisy per-run held-out hid that.
- Hybrid. PPO beats ES from random at equal wall time on nibble, corridor and minefield (docs/rl-grad-notes.md), so stage A (basic foraging from random) should use PPO. ES suits stage B because the Live-like objective (net intake, rank against the cast) is non-differentiable and noisy. The final stage is selection in the true objective: a short Live-config screen, or letting the Ecosystem itself select (drop the RL pool into a Live-config world and harvest the genomes of the lineages alive at +45), which is "RL warm start, then evolution in the target".
- Proposed recipe (unverified as a whole).
  1. Stage A, PPO from random, basics (lean-A set), 20-40 min.
  2. Stage B, ES with rivals = the current Live cast, on Live-like envs: 180 s episodes, wide worlds with food at the post-crash level, a winter phase (low regrowth), and reward = food energy eaten minus energy spent (net income) instead of survive + end energy. Drop wait and lean-times from founder training, or weight them down.
  3. Measure on one fixed benchmark: envs never trained on (own ids, other layouts and sizes), 24+ seeds, mean and standard error, the shipped founders scored as reference rows in the same run. Report the best-of-last-3-checks mean, not the last check.
  4. Behaviour gates before any Live check: env speed at least that of the shipped founder in its role (about 55), net intake per minute at or above the cast's.
  5. Selection: a 45-min Live screen (scale 0.5, 2 seeds, with the cast) ranks candidates; the pool is then the top lineages harvested from that world, not perturbations ranked on 12 seeds.
  6. Upkeep last: once a lean and a big brain behave alike, the lean one wins by its 4 to 25% lower upkeep, so shrink the brain only after the behaviour gate passes.
- Cheapest falsifying test (no new training). lg4 is a lean 1x4 Grazer that is fast in the envs (63-65) and tops all three probe sets. Run the l10-la6 screen with the lg4 pool in place of lg8v2: `node src/founderEval.ts --c Explorer=live:public/live-founders.json:0 --c Oracle=live:public/live-founders.json:2 --c Grazer=fpool:runs/agg/lg4-pool.json --c Aggressor=fpool:runs/agg/la12-pool.json --scale 1 --warmup 0 --copies 10 --energy start --seeds 1 --firstSeed S --minutes 60 --killEnergy 10` for S = 1, 2. The speed account predicts lg4 runs at v above 55 in Live and is alive at +60 in 2 of 2 seeds (lg8v2 was gone by +30 in 2 of 2). If lg4 is fast in Live and still dies by +30, speed is not the gap and the recipe's stage B objective is unsupported; the next suspect is births (energy at birth, cooldown) or the winter. 60 min suffices because every lean failure so far happened by +30 to +60. Proposed, not started (Live checks take hours on this machine).
