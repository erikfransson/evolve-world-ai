# T1: why does backprop (PPO) seem to learn little? (C1a)

Append-only. Probe: `docs/rl-notes/probes/ppoProbe.ts`, run from `src/typescript` as `node ../../docs/rl-notes/probes/ppoProbe.ts --env E --from F --seconds S --seed N` (flags as rlGrad, plus `--criticLr`, `--check`).
heldDet = mean action (the brain as is) on 32 held-out seeds, as rlGrad and the Arena. heldStoch = the training policy with its noise on the same seeds (fixed noise seeds). ev = critic explained variance against Monte Carlo returns of the update's episodes, before the update.

## Log

- 2026-10-21. Read ppo.ts. PPO with Gaussian on pre-tanh turn and thrust, state-free log-std (init -0.5), advantages normalised per update (ppo.ts:604-610), critic regresses raw returns (ppo.ts:670), grad norm 0.5 per net (ppo.ts:730), action held `repeat` ticks (ppo.ts:449-464). The bp-investigate worktree change (noise as an offset, brain every tick) is not adopted here: ticks 2 and 3 of a hold would then play actions whose mean the sample's logp does not describe, which biases the gradient.
- 2026-10-21. Act-path checks (`--check`, 8 seeds): the act path gives the same held-out reward as the plain path (nibble grazer 3.574 both, explorer 3.832 both, random:1 0.25 both; rivals grazer 5.125 both), and the policy net's mean matches the brain (max |tanh(u) - brain output| 1e-4 for founders, from fastTanh; 2e-7 for random). So training and held-out play the same world and the same brain.
- 2026-10-21. Founders are saturated: mean |u| (pre-tanh turn, thrust) grazer 6.4, 4.4 on nibble and 2.5, 5.9 on rivals; explorer 4.6, 4.1 on nibble. 78% (grazer, nibble), 77% (explorer), 67% (grazer, rivals) of the outputs have |u| > 2. A fresh design (random:1) has mean |u| 0.06, 0.26, none above 2. With the noise std 0.6 in pre-tanh space (ppo.ts:458), a saturated action barely moves (tanh(6 - 0.6) = 0.99996), so the founder explores only on the ~20-30% of ticks near a decision edge, and the gradient of a saturated output is mostly noise. Hypothesis H1: PPO from a founder learns little because its outputs are saturated, not because of a bug. Falsified if desaturating (dividing the turn and thrust rows by k, held-out nearly unchanged) does not make PPO improve the founder.
- 2026-10-21. Batch A, nibble, PPO defaults (16 episodes per update, lr 0.003, logstd -0.5, repeat 3), 45 s training, 32 held-out seeds, every 10 updates. Desaturation alone costs held-out (grazer, 32 seeds): k 1 3.43, k 2 3.34, k 3 3.28, k 5 3.05.

  Readings. (1) From a fresh design PPO learns a lot: held-out 0.13 -> 3.04 in both seeds, most of it in the first 40 updates (~8 s). (2) From a founder the measurement artefact is real and large: the founder's own policy with training noise scores 2.14 (grazer) and 2.20 (explorer) against 3.43 and 3.76 deterministic, so a training curve near 2.2 says nothing about learning. (3) Even so, the founder's deterministic held-out moves little: +0.28, +0.24, +0.01, -0.22 after ~300 updates; evaluation-to-evaluation wobble is about +-0.15, so only the grazer gains are likely real. (4) The founders' updates are rougher than the fresh design's: approx KL 0.02-0.12 against ~0.01, clip fraction up to 0.4. (5) Critic explained variance stays 0.1-0.6 for all starts, mediocre but not broken, and not worse for founders. (6) Training noise hurts a founder (3.43 -> 2.14) far more than a fresh design gains from it, although 78% of its outputs are saturated, so saturation does not shield it: the remaining ticks near decision edges, or the 3-tick hold, carry the damage (batch B separates the two).
- 2026-10-21. Batch B (nibble, grazer, 45 s). Desaturated by 3 (start 3.28): -> 3.66 and 3.65 (seeds 1, 2), approx KL ~0.01 early against 0.02-0.03 undesaturated, std falls to ~0.2. It recovers the desaturation loss and ends where the plain founder ends (3.71, 3.67), not above. So desaturation makes the updates smoother but the founder on nibble sits near what this body reaches in 45 s; nibble cannot show whether a founder can improve. lr 0.001 (seed 1): 3.43 -> 3.43 (flat; KL 0.005-0.012), so a smaller step does not help. The logstd runs failed (CLI needs `--logstd=-1.5` for negative values) and were rerun in batch C.
- 2026-10-21. Batch C, part 1: what costs the founder its score under the training policy (nibble, grazer, 32 held-out seeds, `--seconds 0`). Deterministic, brain every tick: 3.43. Noise std 0.6 but no hold (repeat 1): 3.36. No noise (logstd -5) and repeat 1: 3.28. No noise but the 3-tick hold (repeat 3): 2.30. Std 0.22 with the hold: 2.34. A fresh design (random:1) with the hold and no noise: 0.125, equal to its deterministic score. **The 3-tick action hold, not the exploration noise, costs the founder 1.1 of its 3.4.** The founders were trained to steer every tick (bang-bang, saturated outputs); at 10 Hz they overshoot. So PPO with repeat 3 trains a 10 Hz controller and Held-out scores a 30 Hz one: a train/test mismatch that hits founders and not fresh designs (which have no 30 Hz skill to lose). The 0.07-0.15 between 3.43, 3.36 and 3.28 is within the wobble of a chaotic episode (unverified).
- 2026-10-21. Batch C, part 2: from the grazer on harder small envs (defaults, repeat 3, 40 s, 2 seeds) PPO makes held-out worse, not better: corridor 2.47 -> 2.01, 2.49; minefield 3.47 -> 2.71, 3.07; far-food 3.58 -> 3.26, 3.60. heldStoch starts at 1.27 (corridor), 1.66 (minefield), 1.36 (far-food), half the deterministic score, and climbs only to 1.25-1.77. Nibble with std 0.22 (`--logstd=-1.5`, seed 1): 3.43 -> 3.51, KL up to 0.12 (KL grows as 1/std^2 for the same mean shift). So "learns little" from a founder is a real failure, mostly not a measurement artefact; the likely cause is the hold mismatch above (tested in batch D with repeat 1).
- 2026-10-21. Batch D. The hold cost holds across envs and founders (no noise, repeat 3, against deterministic): grazer corridor 2.47 -> 1.14, minefield 3.47 -> 1.62, far-food 3.58 -> 1.46; explorer nibble 3.76 -> 2.26 at repeat 3, 3.34 at repeat 2, while noise std 0.6 at repeat 1 costs it only 3.76 -> 3.71.
  With repeat 1 the artefact is gone: train, heldStoch and heldDet agree (nibble grazer train 3.3-3.7, heldDet 3.4-3.7). But the founder still does not get clearly better in 40-45 s (~120-220 updates): nibble 3.43 -> 3.66, 3.59; corridor 2.47 -> 2.82, 2.25; minefield 3.47 -> 3.13, 2.89. Critic explained variance is higher at repeat 1 on nibble (0.43-0.69). On minefield heldStoch rises (2.67 -> 2.86, 2.98) while heldDet falls: PPO improves the noisy policy it samples, and the mean of that policy need not beat the founder's tuned deterministic play.

- 2026-10-21. Conclusions so far. (a) "Learns little" has two parts. From a fresh design it is false: PPO learns fast. From a founder it is mostly a train/test mismatch: `repeat: 3` (PPO_DEFAULTS, ppo.ts:34) holds each action 3 ticks in training while held-out and the Ecosystem run the brain every tick, and founders, trained by ES at 30 Hz with saturated bang-bang outputs, lose 30-60% of their score at 10 Hz. Training then optimises a 10 Hz controller from a broken start; the Arena does the same, since `backpropOptions` (worker/arenaRun.ts:26) passes only lr and episodes. (b) H1 (saturation blocks learning) is not supported: desaturating changed the update size, not the endpoint, and at repeat 1 the saturated founder improves. (c) Remaining real failures: minefield-like envs where noise is lethal (no KL early stop, one update jumped KL 1.24), and evaluation noise (32 seeds, and 16 on rivals, cannot resolve fine-tuning gains). (d) Unverified: whether the bp-investigate offset variant (noise held, brain every tick) matches repeat 1 on founders while keeping repeat 3's exploration for fresh designs; its gradient is biased (the held noise sits on top of means the logp does not include), so test it rather than assume it.
