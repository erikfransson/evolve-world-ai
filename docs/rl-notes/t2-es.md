## Log

- 2026-10-21. Code read (rl-notes at 31c5076).
  - Defaults `es.ts:14`: sigma 0.08, lr 0.02, decay 0.005, zscore shaping, pairs 16 (Arena: pop 100 = 50 pairs, 1 episode, `arenaForm.ts:77`). Sigma and lr are constant: no schedule anywhere in `es.ts` or `trainer.ts`.
  - Antithetic pairs (`es.ts:59-67`) and common random numbers: every candidate plays the same fresh seeds each step (`trainer.ts:154`, `:228`), so seed difficulty cancels inside a pair. A pair with equal rewards adds nothing (`es.ts:81`).
  - Update is Adam on the ES gradient (`es.ts:88-92`). With bias correction the first step is exactly lr x sign(grad) per weight, and later steps stay near lr per weight whatever the gradient SNR, since Adam normalises each coordinate. lr/sigma = 0.25, so each step moves every weight a quarter of the perturbation scale.
  - Weight decay sits inside the Adam gradient (`es.ts:89`). The zscore gradient per weight has size about 1/(sigma sqrt(2 pairs)) = 1.25 at pop 100, against decay x |theta| = 0.005 x O(1). So the L2 pull is under 1% of the gradient and is no real regulariser (estimate, not measured).
  - Multi-env score is the plain mean of raw per-env rewards (`trainer.ts:164`), so envs with a big reward scale (Live-like, 5 to 20) dominate the zscore and the gradient over small ones (Fence, Minefield, about 1). No per-env normalisation.
  - "Best" (`trainer.ts:238-242`) is the max of 2 x pairs candidates, each on `episodes` seeds (Arena: 1). Even for equal genomes the max of 100 noisy draws sits about 2.5 sd above the mean, so the best-vs-mean gap of issue #54 is first of all an order statistic.
  - Final genome: rlTrain and the Arena keep theta (the mean) of the last iteration (`rlTrain.ts:106`, `arenaRun.ts:135`), not the best candidate and not the best held-out checkpoint. The Arena tiles show the best candidate (`arenaRun.ts:127`, `:133`), so the creature on screen is the luckiest perturbation, not the one that is saved.
  - Arena held-out is 4 seeds per env every 5 iterations (`arenaRun.ts:18-19`).

- 2026-10-21. Budget split at 100 episodes per iteration, rlTrain nibble random:1, 60 it, held-out 32 seeds (`probes/run-split.sh`, logs `split-*.log`). End held-out s1 / s2: pairs 50 x 1 episode 2.74 / 2.21, 25 x 2 2.84 / 1.72, 10 x 5 2.27 / 2.35. Means 2.47, 2.28, 2.31, inside the run-to-run spread (single 32-seed checks differ by up to 0.6).   - Expectation (standard ES variance argument, not measured here): at a fixed episode budget the evaluation-noise part of the gradient variance is the same for any pairs x episodes split, while the direction-sampling part shrinks with more pairs, so more pairs at 1 episode should win for the gradient. More episodes per candidate only pay when the shaping is nonlinear in the noise (ranks, zscore of skewed rewards) or for selection and reporting. This is why issue #54's "10 to 100 episodes per candidate" is better spent on the held-out check and on picking the final genome than on training candidates.
