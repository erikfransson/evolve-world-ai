# Changes

What changed in each version, newest first, so release notes for any range (say 0.1.1 to 0.2.0) can be put together from here.
Each release is a pull request from `development` into `main`, tagged `vX.Y.Z` on `main`.
Add notes under "Unreleased" as work lands on `development`, and turn that heading into the version number at release time.

## Unreleased

- Live: brains cost no energy and eyes half as much (`creature.neuronCost` 0, was 0.005; `creature.rayCost` 0.01, was 0.02), so the Oracle survives beside the Grazer. The Ecosystem and Arena keep their costs.
- Live: three starter founders, Juggernaut, Oracle and Grazer. The Explorer is no longer seeded or offered in "Add your creature" and the RL Arena's Founders choice (one shared file).

## 0.2.7 (2026-10-26)

- Live: room for 5000 creatures (was 3000, #63), births still stop 100 below it. The brain-weight budget stays at 7 million, so a world of big brains still waits for deaths before that. A full world fits fewer viewers (about 16 to 23).
- Live: food regrows 5% slower (`eco.foodRegrow` 0.00285, was 0.003).
- Live: the add panel starts at 3 copies instead of 1 (#65). Bounds are unchanged.

## 0.2.6 (2026-10-26)

- RL Arena: the reward graph draws the luckiest line in its own smaller panel below, so train mean and held-out get a y scale fitted to them and small held-out changes are visible (#54).
- Live, Ecosystem and Trials: the terrain has the abyssal look (#50): a blue-black silt floor, top-lit basalt rock with an edge line, silt-shelf mud, pale sand firm ground and soft haze tints for rich, dry and poison regions (`noiseTex.ts`, `abyssRock.ts`). Drawn ground still equals sensed ground. The simulation is unchanged.

## 0.2.5 (2026-10-26)

- RL: `node src/rlProof.ts` trains several runs and scores each run's last weights on fixed paired held-out seeds against the pre-training copy, random designs and trivial policies, with bootstrap 95% intervals (docs/rl-notes.md).
- RL: episodes in worlds of 32 creatures or fewer run about 12% faster (the settle and birth scans skip the WASM call there); results are unchanged.

## 0.2.4 (2026-10-22)

- Live: the first start of 0.2.4 begins a fresh world. Every save of the old 30000 x 18750 map counts as another world's and is kept on disk but not restored, and the old world's admin settings are set aside (`admin-overrides.previous-world.json`).
- Live: the world holds at most 7 million brain weights (`MAX_LIVE_WEIGHTS`), so every save stays near 45 MB and can be written and read back inside the server's 1 GB memory cap. Births wait at it as at the birth reserve, an add past it is refused with the reason, and a reset that would seed past it is refused. The four founders at the most copies a reset allows hold 5.4 million.
- Live admin: `reset --carry-oldest K --carry-name NAME [--carry-from SAVE]` carries K copies of the oldest living creature (of the running world or a listed save) into the fresh world as a starter family, checked as an upload is. `saves` shows the world each save is of.
- Live: the Inland Sea is 50000 x 31250 (was 30000 x 18750), with slightly bigger and fuller slow (mud) and fast (firm) ground, fewer islands in the mud and fewer holes in the dunes (#53, draft `docs/world-maps/tools/map-F5.json`). The food cap is 1.4 (was 1.5) so the items fit the wire. Stones no longer fill the cliff passes. The world starts fresh, since saves of the old map are refused.
- Live and Ecosystem: a far view of a map with very many items draws at most 90 000 food dots a frame (a fixed share, a little denser), and the ground keeps up to 48 MB of baked bitmaps (was 32 MB), so the bigger Inland Sea zooms as smoothly as the old one.
- Live server: a viewer gets a full item set only when its send queue has room for it, and one too slow to ever take its set is dropped after 10 s instead of holding back everyone's frames. The frame-skip level is 1.5 MB (was 1 MB), above a join's full set.
- Trials, Ecosystem and RL Arena share one set of creature and brain sliders (#58): vision range up to 400 (was 300), from 10 in Trials and Arena and from 40 in the Ecosystem, whose grid cells are the ray setting wide, neurons per layer 1 to 32 (were 4 to 32), vision angle and range in steps of 1 (Arena and Trials were 5 and 10), Arena "Sight length" is now "Vision range". Trials item size and mutation rate use the Ecosystem's steps (0.1 and 0.5%). Defaults are unchanged.
- RL Arena: a Time pressure group with three envs, Fading food, Stings and Race (#56). A new reward, early, pays each bite by the share of the episode still left.
- RL Arena: held-out checks use a fixed set of 12 seeds per env instead of 4 (#54); Live visitor training keeps 4. The graph's best line is now "luckiest candidate" (or episode): on held-out worlds that candidate scores about like the current brain.
- `rlTrain --vary F` trains and checks on varied versions of the env (food, poison, rival counts, start energies, world size), for experiments.
- Live World (#55): the selected creature's rays are drawn at the heading they were sensed at. They were drawn at the body's smoothly turning heading while their lengths came from the last detail (up to 0.2 s old), so a fast spinner showed rays whose lengths belonged to other angles and ended off the walls and rock.
- Live World (#55): the selected creature's rays are cast again every frame at its smoothly turning heading against the items, creatures and rock the page draws (`live/rayRecast.ts`, the engine's geometry), so they turn with the body and end where they hit. They were drawn at that heading with lengths from the last detail (up to 0.2 s old), so a fast spinner showed rays whose lengths belonged to other angles. Until the map's rock is known, and with a stale map design, the detail's rays are drawn at the heading they were sensed at.
- Ecosystem and Live World (#55): the heading sent to the renderer is wrapped to [-pi, pi). A creature that spins for a long time had an ever larger heading, and a float32 of it got coarse (about 0.01 rad after a day), so its body tick, nose and rays jittered. Simulation unchanged.
- Live server: an optional founder pools file (`server/founder-pools.json`, server only) gives a fresh world's seeded copies trained variants of each founder instead of clones. No pools ship yet, so worlds seed as before.
- RL Arena and Trials: the training caps are much higher for long runs. Arena iterations 20000 to 200000, population 1000 to 5000, episodes per candidate 4 to 40, episodes per update 64 to 320. Trials population 2000 to 10000. Live World limits are unchanged.
- Live: the kill bonus is 5 (`eco.killEnergy`, was 25 in 0.2.3): at 25 the Juggernaut took over the world. Already set on the running world; a fresh world now starts with it.
- Live and Ecosystem: the selected creature's card shows its kills (creatures that died in a collision it survived, #52). Saves keep the count; older saves read as 0. Protocol 5 (kills in DETAIL and `best`).
- Live and Ecosystem: "Highlight family" turns itself off when the lit family dies out, so the world gets its colours back (#57).
- Live and Ecosystem: a "Most kills" pick next to Oldest and Most children (#61).
- RL Arena: the reward graph moves to the top once training starts and stays after it ends. It has labelled axes (reward, iteration) with ticks, and the legend shows the latest train mean, best and held-out values.

## 0.2.3 (2026-10-16)

- Live admin: the saved settings (`admin-overrides.json`) load key by key instead of all or nothing. A key this version does not know (after a rollback past the version that added it) is skipped with a log line, a refused value is skipped on its own with its reason, and the rest still apply, so a rollback never silently drops standing settings such as `add.aliveMax`. The file is copied to `admin-overrides.ignored.json` whenever anything was skipped.
- Live founders: the Grazer is the 2026-10-16 retrain (b3's grazer-vj, same body and name), trained 3 h against the other founders in Live-like envs; without a kill bonus it beats the shipped Juggernaut in 4 of 4 seeds at 180 min while the Juggernaut survives, and it survived 3 of 3 three-founder seeds at bonus 25. A restored world keeps its creatures; the new Grazer seeds the next fresh world (reset or new map).
- Training in Add your creature waits while the tab is hidden (and ends with its best result after 10 min hidden in all, counting every hidden stretch) and uses at most 3 helper threads. A run that ended because the tab stayed hidden never adds on its own: the result is kept, the remaining training is skipped, and the add goes in when the visitor presses Add again.
- Live: a kill gives 25 energy on top of the loser's (`eco.killEnergy` 25, was 0), so hunting pays a little. The world rules say so, and the admin can change it live (`eco.killEnergy`, 0 to 300). A restored world keeps its saved value until the admin sets it.

## 0.2.2 (2026-10-16)

- RL envs (#44): duels against a scripted charger (hit it when it is weak, dodge it when it is strong), per-episode augmentation of energies, rival counts, items and sizes, fast ground, and a kill bonus. New engine setting `eco.killEnergy` (bonus energy for a kill, default 0, so worlds are unchanged).
- Live and Ecosystem: smoother motion and fewer slow frames (#49). Creatures keep moving through a server frame that arrives late (each frame's motion spans 1.3x the usual gap, which is now an average over recent frames instead of the last one alone): while following one, the frame-to-frame change in camera speed halves under real tunnel jitter. Creature dots are stamped from one bitmap atlas, at whole device pixels with quarter-pixel placement, instead of filled as paths of a thousand circles: at whole-world view, frames over 25 ms drop from 6-38% to 0-5% on an Iris Xe.
- Trials: "Save best creature" downloads the best creature of the newest generation as a creature file (`trials-gen42-fitness123.json`) that loads in Live's "Add your creature" and the RL Arena's "Start from"; it behaves as it did in Trials (#47).
- Live: "Add your creature" says under the training toggles that more advanced RL training can be done in the RL Arena, with a link (#45).
- Live admin: `npm run admin -- reset [--copies N]` (`./live reset` in the admin wrapper; the server exits 75 and the unit starts it again) starts a fresh world with N copies of each founder (default 10, at most 725 for 4 founders), keeping the old world's saves and settings in a `before-reset-<time>` folder that is never restored. The page shows the seed count up to 3000.
- The map is drawn once per zoom around the view and copied each frame, instead of drawing every rock outline again each frame. On a mid laptop (Iris Xe) the Live page at whole-world view goes from about 5 to 20-60 frames a second (the rest is the creature dots and items), and at 2x zoom from 12-20 to 60; the Ecosystem page gains the same way.

## 0.2.1 (2026-10-16)

### Live World
- "Add your creature" adds up to 10 copies (1, 2, 3, 5 or 10; was 1 to 3), #42. A visitor may add three full adds back to back (`add.burst` 3), at most 30 creatures per 5 minutes (`add.perVisitor`; per connection until the tunnel's address is checked), 50 for everyone together (`add.perWindow`), and at most 50 visitor-added creatures alive at once (`add.aliveMax`, which replaces the 10% share and the floor of 20; an older overrides file's `add.aliveShare` and `add.aliveFloor` are skipped with a log line). An add past the room is told how many copies still fit. Until the tunnel's visitor address is checked (`LIVE_TRUST_FORWARDED=1`), one client with two connections can take the whole 50 of a window and fill the 50 alive, shutting others out until those creatures die; all of these are admin-tunable, to tighten when traffic grows. Jumping to a creature zooms in 1.5x less close.
  Release order: the site first, then `./live promote`; a page from before this version shows adding as off against the new server until it is reloaded.
- Poison is no longer lethal on Live: a bite costs 50 energy (one food's worth); admin-tunable as `world.poisonDamage` (100 or more kills, `./live get` says so).
- Upgrading a running Live world: a world restored from a 0.2.0 save keeps its saved settings (lethal poison, birth ranges 100-225, 50-110, 5-20, the old starter names); only a fresh world starts with the new values. To bring a running world in line, after the promote: `./live set world.poisonDamage=50 eco.birthEnergyRange=100,500 eco.birthCostRange=50,250 eco.birthCooldownRange=5,40`, `./live rename Pathfinder Explorer` and `./live rename GentleGrazer Grazer` (each should report 10 families, fewer if some died out; add `--force` only if a visitor family already carries the new name). Pages already open show the new poison text after a reload.
- "Add your creature": birth energy up to 500, cost up to 250, cooldown up to 40 s (the Live world's own ranges, so children drift within them too), and a mutation strength slider (×0.25 to ×4, its family inherits it).
- The lineage list counts all families of one name as one row (the 10 seeded copies of a starter, a visitor's copies), and lighting it lights all of them. A name a living family carries, or a starter's, is refused for a new add. Admin `rename <from> <to>` (the old name exactly as shown; a new name another living family carries, in any case, is refused unless `--force`). Starters renamed Explorer and Grazer.
- Legend: grouped (ground, regions, items, creatures) with swatches cut from the map itself and the effect of each, with numbers; the Ecosystem and Trials legends use the same swatches.
- Folded cards shrink to small chips at the edge, so with everything folded the whole world is in view.
- Camera: with the side cards' offset (after selecting a creature) every edge of the world can be dragged into view.
- Ecosystem creatures carry a mutation factor (`mutationScale`, default 1, 0.25 to 4 on insert) that multiplies the mutation std when they have children; children inherit it unchanged. Saves hold it only for creatures that differ from 1, older saves restore as 1. Runs at the default 1 are bit-identical.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-15)

### Live World
- Landing page polish: a hero with buttons to watch the Live World or create your own, a Live World card that shows the real Inland Sea from above with six lineages grazing, giving birth and dying, its live numbers (creatures alive, people watching) from the server, a busier Ecosystem card, a short "How it works" (sense, think, evolve), a fuller footer, and share previews (description, Open Graph and Twitter tags, a 1200 x 630 image). The Trials card's rays no longer reach past the arena wall.
- Live server: `GET /stats` answers `{"creatures", "viewers"}` for the landing page (public, cacheable for 10 s, rebuilt at most once a second, 5 requests a second for all callers with a burst of 20, past that a 503; every other plain request still gets a 426).
- Live World moves to The Inland Sea (30000 x 18750) with the launch food values (food worth 50, slower regrowth, cap 1.5, scarce poison), a one hour year and 3x speed, at most 3000 creatures with births stopping 100 below that so visitors can still add creatures. A fresh world starts with 10 of each starter founder; saves of the old world are not restored and are deleted by the usual rotation after 4 days. The page draws the map (built on a worker from its name), and the info section lists its regions with their food and poison.
- `eco.birthReserve`: births stop this many creatures below `maxPopulation` while inserted creatures may still fill up to it (default 0; admin-tunable on Live up to 1000), so Live visitors can add creatures after a boom.
- Live World: an extinct world stays empty until visitors add creatures again (admin `reseed=1` brings back the restart), at least 20 added creatures may be alive however small the population (`add.aliveFloor`), and children change colour less (3 degrees, was 8) so families keep their colour. An empty world still shows its map, with a note and a button to add a creature.
- Live World: the page opens on a card to watch the world or add your own creature. "Add your creature" picks one of four founders (or a creature file from the RL Arena), tunes its vision, brain and birth traits with a live energy-per-second readout, can train it for 30 s in the browser, and adds 1 to 3 named copies; the page then follows one and lights its family. The server caps adds (20 creatures per 5 minutes for everyone, 3 per visitor once the tunnel's visitor address is checked, added creatures at most 10% of the population); added creatures start with 300 energy and have their first child no sooner than 60 s after they arrive (admin settings). Protocol 4: names, u32 founder ids. Below the world, an info section with the legend, ground shares and the world's rules, and a footer with the version and source link.
- Live server, from the release review: an admin reseed seeds the starter founders and never rebuilds every tick when it gives an empty world; startup refuses to start a fresh world over saves of this world that fail to restore (it exits so systemd tries again, instead of a new save that rotation would let replace the real world); a birth reserve at or past the population cap is refused; the add limits run on the monotonic clock, so a wall clock step back cannot cut a visitor off.
- Live, from the release review: startup skips other worlds' saves and restores this world's older ones (a test-map run and a revert no longer start fresh); an alive floor with no share keeps adding on; adds are not refused as busy for the first second after a start or resume; the add panel's sliders stay off until a founder or file is there; the hello carries the map's version, and a page with other map data draws plain ground and says "Map updating".
- Live admin: `blank-name <name>` clears an unfit visitor name from every family that carries it. Overrides that no longer apply at startup are kept in `admin-overrides.ignored.json`. A save made on other data of the same map counts as another world.
- Live World starter founders are now trained: Pathfinder (ranges far), Juggernaut (rams weaker creatures), Oracle (the biggest brain) and Gentle Grazer (never fights), replacing the four pool picks; they seed a fresh world and are offered in "Add your creature" and the RL Arena.
- Live page states: a server still on the older protocol (the page released before the server) shows "Updating" and keeps retrying instead of asking for a reload, which is left for a server newer than the page. The info section stays hidden until the world has sent its data, and on phones the status pill reads in full.
- Live World saves no longer keep a lineage row for every visitor-inserted founder: a runtime lineage is dropped when its last member dies, and older saves drop such rows on load.

### Maps and terrain
- Ecosystem settings: with a map chosen, the world width and height sliders are disabled (the map sets the size). Dragging one used to crash the render worker.
- Ecosystem page: choosing The Inland Sea builds it once, on a worker, and hands the arrays to the drawing side and to the run, instead of the render worker and the simulation worker each building it (the render side took about 4.5 s, and froze the page for that long when it draws itself). The preview says "Building the map" until the map arrives, and if that build fails the run sends its own arrays. The Trials simulation worker no longer bundles the map builder and specs (21 kB smaller); the Trials page itself still loads them.
- Ecosystem fixes on The Inland Sea: the legend now lists Rock, Mud, Firm ground and the poison and rich food tints (it read the random-terrain densities, which are 0 on a map), and Start no longer makes every helper thread build the map (several seconds each, which could trip the start timeout and drop to one thread): the coordinator sends the built arrays and the results are unchanged. Time to first frame in headless Chrome on a busy machine went from 50 s (helpers failed) to 26 s.
- Rock outlines are about 15 times faster: on the Inland Sea they took 15.3 s on the main thread and now about 1 s, and less when only some are in view, because each body is traced when first drawn. The result is the same.
- The Inland Sea look: cliff and massif edges read as broken rock (no round bumps), stones and Drowned Town outcrops are angular, and the Ashfall poison shore has a subtle tint.
- Hand-designed maps: `world.map` names a map (`src/sim/maps/`) that replaces the random stones, mud and firm ground. A map brings its own ground raster, rock, and food and poison weights: items spawn and regrow in proportion to the weights, with local caps per 1000 unit cell, and founders favour rich places. Saves store only the name. The first map is The Inland Sea (30000 x 18750), an Ecosystem preset with the launch food values. Worlds without a map are unchanged bit for bit. The ground outline no longer breaks where the field is exactly one half between two raster cells.
- Terrain polish: mud and firm ground are a smooth field on a coarse raster instead of unions of circles, so patches have organic, ragged outlines at the same coverage and patch size settings. The edge is the exact 0.5 contour of that field, which is where creatures sense the ground (the field is also cheaper to build). Mud is a wet-earth texture with a smooth lighter shallow rim, firm ground is dry, packed earth with grit and a crisp edge. Ground is drawn from cached bitmaps from afar, so whole-world frames are cheap. Rock gets a shadowed south cliff face. Maps with mud or firm ground generate differently than before (rock and map-off runs are unchanged).
- Maps drawn from afar (the Inland Sea at scales 0.04 and up): the biggest ground patches are cut into 640 px bitmap tiles made when first in view, one bitmap per frame while the rest is painted live, and every zoom tier keeps its own bitmaps (up to 32 MB per map, least recently used tier dropped first). A frame at those scales costs about 15 ms instead of 120 ms, and zooming back is instant. The ground looks the same up to resampling at the edge line.

### Speed
- Speed: The Inland Sea at 10k creatures on one thread went from about 70 to about 41 ms CPU per tick under load (about 35 ms or less on a quiet machine), with results bit-identical:
  - The engine's per-creature work runs in WebAssembly over the resident rows: the physics step (with the engine's own Math.sin and cos), the crowd grid, the collision pair search, the item lookup, and the settle and birth scans. About 1.13x per tick (the coordinator 1.20x with 3 helpers). The Creature code is the reference, and parity, fuzz and mutation-checked tests compare the WebAssembly versions with it bit for bit.
  - Helper threads use the resident creature rows too: with 3 helpers the CPU per tick drops about 1.3x and the coordinator's share from about 37 to 24 ms at 10k creatures on The Inland Sea, results unchanged.
  - Eating walks only the creatures that touch an item and the crowd rebuild skips a species copy: about 1.04x per tick on The Inland Sea, results unchanged.
  - Creature body state lives in rows in WASM memory: about 1.15x per tick on big worlds, results unchanged, about 7% less memory.
  - WASM memory now grows by at least an eighth at a time instead of page by page (each grow copied the whole memory): about 1.14x per tick on The Inland Sea at 10k creatures, results unchanged, about 10% more memory.
  - Faster simulation on big maps (about 1.08x on The Inland Sea at 10k creatures, results unchanged): one array-based collision pass for every mode, item lookups in grid order, keepOut skips rock where none is near, and a faster birth scan.

### RL Arena
- RL Arena: "Start from" has a Founders choice with the four Live World starter founders (#39), so a founder can be trained further and saved or added to Live from its file.
- RL Arena: a new "Bigger" env group after Advanced with four Live-like worlds (1000 to 2200 wide, 90 s, regrowing food, lethal poison, 8 to 25 seed pool foragers, bumps hurt, start energy 300): Live patch, Feast and famine, Poison country and Crowded commons.
- RL Arena fix: rams now count. A rival killed by bumping into the learner never raised the learner's energy (it gets the rival's energy from before the hit, at most the damage it took), so the ram reward in Aggressor and Brawl never paid out. A ram is now a rival that dies touching the learner, and no other rival, with energy left the tick before (a rival eating poison while touching the learner also counts).
- RL Arena envs, after a review of all of them (three bodies, two seeds, every trained creature scored on every env): two new memory envs, Glimpse (a food patch shown for 1.5 s) and Truffles (food never visible), and Flash now has 5 bites, so the Memory group has three envs that each need memory over 6 seeds. Wait for it joins Energy. Zigzag and Busy meadow are gone, as near-copies of Four rooms with Fence and of Live patch. Brawl moves to Fight, and the four Advanced foragers to Find food, Avoid, Compete and Energy; Advanced keeps only the hunt envs.
- RL rams: a rival that starves, is killed by another rival, or dies of poison beside the learner no longer pays a ram (no ram pays on a tick when any creature ate poison), and one dying on its first tick is judged too; a test for each rule. Aggressor and Brawl re-measured, the Bigger envs measured (`docs/rl-envs.md`). Env grouping is decided only in `envs.ts` (the unused `envGroups` is gone, the gallery's folded groups come from `FOLDED_GROUPS`), the Bigger envs use a typed `bigger()` helper, and the Arena's seed pool select is `poolEntry`, apart from the Live founders.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-12)

First numbered release, covering everything built so far.
- Modes: Trials, Ecosystem, The Great Hunt (predators and prey), RL Arena, and Live World (one shared world on a server, watched by everyone).
- World: food and poison with regrowth, seasons, obstacles (rock boulders and ridges) and terrain (mud and firm ground) with a terrain brain input.
- Creatures: ray vision, evolved brains with short and long memory, heritable birth traits, lineages and records.
- Engine: WebAssembly sense and brain kernels, helper threads, render worker, Gentle on CPU; bit-identical results across threads.
- Live World: versioned saves (format 2), protocol 3, sandboxed server with abuse-tested limits.
- Live World shows the release it runs ("Evolve World vX.Y.Z" under the side panel), and the server's own version when it differs during a deploy.
- Live World map proposals for #36 in `docs/world-maps/` (design only, not built).
- Obstacles redrawn: stones are angular faceted boulders and ridges as ranges of rock slabs with loose pebbles, lit from the upper left, with the drawn edge exactly where creatures bounce. Mud and firm ground lose their ripple and chevron marks (the legend names them), and Trials, Ecosystem and The Great Hunt get a size setting for each (Mud patch size, Firm patch size). A browser save made with the map on restores with the new rock shapes.
- RL Arena: a page left open across a redeploy says to reload instead of "the training worker crashed".
