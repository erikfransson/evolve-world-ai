# V8 engine costs at 20000 to 30000 width

Branch worktree-agent-a57f1551ab260b6e3. Bench: `node src/ecoBench.ts --width 20000 --seconds 20 --seed 1`, 600 ticks, fingerprint 179585160. Load average 14 to 28 on 8 cores, so absolute ms are inflated. CPU ms per tick (`cpuMsPerTick`) is the metric.

| Idea | Before | After | Speedup | Exact? | Peak RSS change | Lines added | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Insertion sort instead of `Float64Array.sort` in `SenseBatch.sortBySlot` | 22.67 ms median (3 pairs) | 24.25 ms median | 0.93x (slower) | Yes, fingerprint equal | not measured | +9 | Reverted. Batch order is not nearly sorted enough for insertion sort to win. |

## Costs that are real

Share of samples from `node --prof` (19526 ticks, 20 s run):

- WASM sense kernels (`wasm-function[0..2]`): about 46%. This is the work itself.
- JS per tick, each 4 to 6%: `Ecosystem.finishTick` (remember and steer), `senser.run` (copies crowd and creature state into WASM memory, reads each `Creature` object), `Crowd.rebuild` (reads one `CreatureState` object per creature), `sharedPhase`, `senseKernels.run`, `collideAll`.
- `eatAt` 3%, `stepBody` 2.5%.
- Node builtin `TypedArrayPrototypeSort` inside `sortBySlot`: 1.5%.
- JS to WASM call wrapper: about 1%.

The cost in `senser.run` and `Crowd.rebuild` is pointer chasing over one heap object per creature, which is the struct-of-arrays point already noted in `layout.md` and `wasm.md`. It is not a JIT problem.

## Costs that are noise

- Deoptimisations: 110 in the 20 s run, with reasons "insufficient type feedback" (51 generic named access, 6 compare, others), "dependent field type constness changed" (13), "dependent prototype chain changed" (11), "allocation site tenuring changed" (8). They cluster at warm-up. No function was seen deoptimising repeatedly in the steady state.
- Element transitions: `--trace-elements-transitions` gave 8434 `PACKED_SMI -> PACKED_ELEMENTS` events in a 3 s run and 8442 in an 8 s run. The count does not grow with time, so they are module load, not the tick.
- Megamorphic or polymorphic inline caches: `LoadIC` and `KeyedLoadIC` miss handlers are 0.8% of samples. The IC log showed no steady-state megamorphic site in the sim files.
- Closures per tick: none in the hot path. `findIndex` runs only in `refreshDetail` (on selecting a creature), and `onFounder` is built once.
- Hidden class changes: the `Creature`, `EcoCreature`, and `CreatureState` shapes are set in constructors. The deopts above are field representation changes during warm-up.
- GC: 18 pauses in the 20 s run, summing to about 0.4 s wall. Most come while the heap grows from 9 MB to 100 MB in the first 10 s. Steady scavenges are 5 to 9 ms. The `--prof` sampler counted GC at only 0.6%, so the wall-clock share is larger than the sampler suggests. A 32 MB young generation cut the sum to 0.37 s, which is within run-to-run noise on CPU per tick.

## Verdict

No JS-engine fix worth merging. The sort change is slower and was reverted. The young-generation flag does nothing measurable in Node and cannot be set in a browser. The remaining JS cost is object layout (the struct-of-arrays work in `wasm.md`), not engine behaviour.
