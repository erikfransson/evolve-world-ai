## Where the tick is (3200 seeded, 3300 steady, 13.7-14.3 ms)

`kernelTimers.ts --copies 800` (ms per tick): brain forward 4.51, sense 4.27, step 1.22, fill 0.50 + prepare 0.31 + pack 0.26, touch 0.49, crowd rebuild 0.65 (1.32 calls) + refresh 0.09 + sweep 0.18, syncItems 0.34 (patchCell 0.235), eat loop 0.27, giveBirth 0.14, regrow 0.09, settle 0.08.
`--cpu-prof` (250 us, steady ticks only): two wasm functions are 55% of the samples, JS outside the kernels about 6%, GC 2% (138 scavenges in 40 s, 3-4 ms each, about 47 KB allocated per tick), `process.cpuUsage` 3% is the bench's own `--profile` timer. No JS function is above 0.3 ms per tick except `patchCell`. Nothing runs outside the profiled phases.

## Verdict

The tick is flat: the two big kernels hold 63% and everything else is a list of 0.1-0.3 ms items, each already cut or shown flat. The only exact item with a repeatable gain is the sin/cos port (0.7-1.0%); it does not clear the 1% bar alone and needs a probe. The one unmeasured lever is huge pages (brain forward is a 24 MB stream through 4 KB pages), which needs a host that can give 2 MB blocks. Prototype patch for the trig and exp ports: scratchpad `trig-exp-proto.patch` (not committed, kernels not regenerated on the branch).
