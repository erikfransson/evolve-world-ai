## Now (after release 0.2.0)
- A1 Terrain and obstacle look: polish rock, ridges, mud and firm ground so they look refined when zoomed in, in the style of the map drafts in `docs/world-maps/`. Creatures sense the ground under them through the `terrain` input (-1 mud, 0 normal, 1 firm); keep what is drawn and what is sensed identical. Done (0.2.0): mud and firm are a smooth raster field (`groundField.ts`, domain-warped noise blobs, no disc unions), `valueAt` reads the field and `groundScene.ts` draws its exact 0.5 contour (test: `groundScene.test.ts`), so the look is organic and drawn equals sensed Rock got a south cliff. The field can later carry food and poison weights (A2, biome raster). Open: a fresh-eyes visual review.
- A2 Live World map: phase 1 built (engine only, 0.2.0): `world.map` (`src/sim/maps/`), the Inland Sea spec scaled to 30000 x 18750 (Erik 2026-10-15, was 40000 x 25000; geography x0.75, stone and edge detail unchanged) from the draft by `docs/world-maps/tools/to-engine.mjs`, weighted spawning, local food and poison caps on 1000 unit cells, rock discs from the spec, an Ecosystem preset, saves rebuild from the name. Cost at the old 40000 x 25000: 15.8 CPU ms per tick against 18.9 for a Highlands-style world of the same size (details in `docs/perf-notes.md`). Open (phase 2): per-region trait stats in the viewer, a close-up look check of the rock. Live use: A7.
- A5 Live World food (Erik, 2026-10-12): food worth about 50 energy, slower regrowth, a fairly high food cap so food can pile up in quiet places (not extreme), poison scarce except in the map's poison regions; launch, watch and retune live (all admin-tunable). Launch values at 30000 x 18750 in `server/src/config.ts`: foodEnergy 50, foodRegrow 0.003, foodMaxDensity 1.5, poisonRegrow 0.0003, poisonMaxDensity 0.01, seasons with yearMinutes 60. At 40000 x 25000 the population was about 800-2300 over the year, 9.8 CPU ms per tick, peak RSS 344 MB (details in `docs/perf-notes.md`). Carrying capacity (2026-10-15, Inland preset, cap raised to 20000, no helpers, 3 simulated years): 30000 x 18750 settles at a mean of 887 (332-1402 over the seasons, first boom peak 2672), 2.2 CPU ms per tick, RSS 346 MB; 40000 x 25000 at 1440 (623-2223, boom 4133), 6.1 ms, 435 MB. Both stay under the Live cap of 3000 after the first boom, so the cap does not hold the population; per area the smaller map is about as dense. Not yet checked for pile-up in ungrazed places.
- A3 Live World page: info section below the world (legend, ground shares from the server, world rules, region table) and the footer are built. The region table fills from the map (`live/mapRegions.ts`, food and poison as the spec weights). Open: the viewer-zoom inset.
- A4 Visitor-inserted creatures: built (0.2.0): start energy 300 and a 60 s first-birth hold, admin settings `add.startEnergy` and `add.birthHoldS` (no engine change: the hold is a last birth placed in the future).
- A6 Inland Sea launch (Erik, 2026-10-12): seed it with 10 of each starter founder (C1), not empty. If everything dies out it stays empty for visitors to repopulate, no restart (`reseed` 0, `add.aliveFloor` 20). Seeding is built: a fresh world (never a restored one) gets 10 copies of each founder in `public/live-founders.json` at random open spots, named after the founder ("Slow and steady" becomes SlowAndSteady), with a pool founder's start energy; they are no visitor adds and show as "starter" (lineage list, selected card), also after a restore. The file is checked like an upload on every start (at most 8 founders, 512 kB), and a bad file stops the server with the reason.
- A7 Inland Sea on the live server (built and reviewed, 0.2.0): `liveConfig` is the Inland Sea with the A5 food values, `speed` 3, `initialPopulation` 0 plus seeding (A6). Item headroom scales with area: the slots stay under 0.9 x `MAX_ITEM_SLOTS` (at the old 40000 x 25000 food 2.3 + poison 0.05 = 235000 slots; at 30000 x 18750 the fixed food 3 + poison 1 fits, 225000 slots), and admin caps are held to that. A save of another world size or map is refused at restore, so the first start after the promote begins a fresh Inland world (saved at once); such saves are skipped (a revert finds this world's older saves), and rotation deletes them after 4 days like any save. A fresh world moves the old `admin-overrides.json` to `admin-overrides.previous-world.json` (one copy), so they never apply to it. The page builds the map from its name on a worker (the hello carries `map.name`). Measured numbers (startup, saves, item sync, page frames, population, cap runs) are in `docs/perf-notes.md`.
  - Cap (Erik, 2026-10-14): 3000, births stop 100 below it (`eco.birthReserve` 100, admin-tunable up to 1000) so visitors can still add. A seeded world sits at 2900 after about 90 simulated seconds.

## Decisions
- 2026-10-12: `main` is the release, all work goes to `development`, releases are pull requests (CLAUDE.md, Git). Versions start at 0.1.0.
- 2026-10-12: The Great Hunt removed; focus on Live and Ecosystem, with Trials and RL Arena for training.
- 2026-10-12: The Inland Sea is the Live World map, built at 5.5x the old area (40000 x 25000), cut to 3.1x (30000 x 18750) on 2026-10-15 (Erik), growing to 10x later if creatures still meet often (#36).
- 2026-10-12: Version history lives in `docs/changes.md`.
- 2026-10-12: Inland Sea launch settings: new food values apply at launch (not to today's world); the world starts with 10 of each starter founder (Erik changed this from "starts empty", 2026-10-12), and an extinct world stays empty for visitors to repopulate; max population 3000 with births stopping 100 below it (Erik, 2026-10-14; admin-adjustable); seasons with a one hour year.
