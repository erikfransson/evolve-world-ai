## Now (2026-10-12)
- A1 Terrain and obstacle look: polish rock, ridges, mud and firm ground so they look refined when zoomed in, in the style of the map drafts in `docs/world-maps/`. Creatures sense the ground under them through the `terrain` input (-1 mud, 0 normal, 1 firm); keep what is drawn and what is sensed identical. Done 2026-10-12 on `polishA1b`: mud and firm are a smooth raster field (`groundField.ts`, domain-warped noise blobs, no disc unions), `valueAt` reads the field and `groundScene.ts` draws its exact 0.5 contour (test: `groundScene.test.ts`), so the look is organic and drawn equals sensed; map-on maps with mud or firm differ from before. Rock got a south cliff. The field can later carry food and poison weights (A2, biome raster). Open: a fresh-eyes visual review.
- A2 Live World map: phase 1 built on branch `inland` (engine only): `world.map` (`src/sim/maps/`), the Inland Sea spec scaled to 40000 x 25000 from the draft by `docs/world-maps/tools/to-engine.mjs`, weighted spawning, local food and poison caps on 1000 unit cells, rock discs from the spec, an Ecosystem preset, saves rebuild from the name. Numbers for the Live session (nice 19, no helpers, 2000 founders, cap 2500, A5 values, 900 simulated seconds, two runs in parallel): map 15.8 CPU ms per tick vs 18.9 for a Highlands-style world of the same size, at mean population about 2200; earlier 2400 s runs at mean 1480: 7.3 vs 6.0 for a plain world. Item slots are exactly density x 100000 (local caps are normalised and weighted by open area): food 1.5 + poison 0.01 = 151000, food 2 + poison 0.05 = 205000. Poison is scarce (base 0.15, Ashfall 3.5): at 0.01 about 52 shore cells hold 517 of the 1000, 486 of 1000 cells hold any, the open sea 0. Food about 10-12k and poison about 930-1000 items in the run. Raster 0.99 MB, map build about 3 s per thread (each thread builds its own), save 13.6 MB at 1200 creatures, peak RSS about 230 MB. Open (phase 2): per-region trait stats in the viewer, a close-up look check of the rock. Live use: built on `live-inland` (A7).
